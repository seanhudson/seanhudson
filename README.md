# Sean Hudson

**Digital innovation & product executive who still builds.**

I work at the intersection of product, technology, customer experience, design, and business strategy — usually where there's an ambiguous problem and something new needs to exist.

I currently lead customer experience, digital product, and innovation initiatives at AAPC. I also remain hands-on with architecture, UX, prototyping, software development, and AI-enabled products.

## What I do

I tend to work from **idea → working product → scale**.

That often means:

- identifying an unmet customer or business need
- rapidly designing and building a proof of concept
- validating whether the idea is worth pursuing
- establishing the architecture and product direction
- transitioning proven concepts to teams for production and scale

I prefer working prototypes and customer evidence over innovation theater.

## How I think about product

My preferred approach is simple:

**Understand the problem → build enough to prove it → learn → scale what works.**

I've spent my career moving between executive leadership and hands-on product work.
